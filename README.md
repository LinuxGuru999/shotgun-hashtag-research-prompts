# Hashtag Research Prompts

Find niche hashtags that actually convert

## Overview
Hashtag Research Prompts is a digital prompt pack for creators who want to organize hashtag research into repeatable workflows. It includes research prompts, niche clustering templates, and a tracking sheet to test and refine tag sets over time. It helps you turn scattered ideas into structured experiments, though results still depend on content quality, platform behavior, and audience fit.

## Use cases
- Planning a new niche series and mapping broad, mid-tier, and long-tail hashtags before posting.
- Refreshing an underperforming content category by grouping tags by theme and comparing reach or saves across posts.
- Managing a creator calendar with a tracking sheet that records which hashtag mixes accompany each post and their early metrics.

## How to use
1. Download the files and open the prompt pack in your preferred notes or AI tool.
2. Run the research prompts for your niche, then sort results into the clustering template.
3. Copy chosen hashtag sets into the tracking sheet and update metrics after each post.

## What is included
- Research prompts
- Niche clustering
- Tracking sheet
